# KASH_INFO - Founding Members Program

**Status:** Approved
**Date:** 2025-10-26
**Total Founding Member Spots:** 600 (50 per sector)

---

## FOUNDING MEMBER DEFINITION

**Founding Members** are the first 600 elite professionals to join kash_info during Phase 1 (Months 1-6).

**Structure:**
- **50 founding spots per sector**
- **12 sectors × 50 = 600 total founding members**
- Hand-picked by project owner and initial board members
- Highest quality bar (set the standard for all future members)

**Purpose:**
- Build the core community foundation
- Test platform features and provide feedback
- Create initial resource library
- Establish quality standards
- Provide social proof and credibility
- Drive word-of-mouth growth

## FOUNDING MEMBER BENEFITS PACKAGE

### 1. Special Badge & Recognition
- **"Founding Member"** badge displayed prominently on profile
- Visual distinction from regular members (gold/platinum color scheme?)
- Listed on platform "About" page: "Meet Our Founding Members"
- Recognized in platform announcements and marketing

### 2. Lifetime Discounted Pricing
- **Founding Member Price: $397/year**
- Regular member price: $497/year (difference: $100/year savings)
- **Grandfather Clause: Price NEVER increases**
  - Even if regular membership goes to $597, $697, or higher
  - Founding members always pay $397/year
  - Locked in for life (as long as membership is active)

**Value Proposition:**
> "Join now as a Founding Member and lock in $397/year forever, while regular members may pay $697+ in the future."

### 3. Lighter Annual Re-Verification
**Regular Members:**
- Must submit substantial proof of value contribution annually
- Full board review every year
- Risk of non-renewal if value not demonstrated

**Founding Members:**
- Simplified annual check-in (not full re-verification)
- "Are you still active and bringing value?" (lighter review)
- Benefit of the doubt (founders trusted unless obvious issues)
- Only lose membership for:
  - Non-payment
  - Serious community guideline violations
  - Complete inactivity (6+ months no engagement)

**Why:** Founding members proved their value by being selected first. Reward their early commitment with trust.

### 4. Unlimited Invitations
**Regular Members:**
- Maximum 5 invitations per member

**Founding Members:**
- **Unlimited invitations**
- Can invite as many qualified people as they want
- Invitees still must pass board approval
- Encourages founding members to recruit other elite professionals

**Tracking:**
- System tracks which founding member invited which new members
- Recognition for members who bring high-quality recruits
- Potential: "Top Recruiter" badge for founding members who bring 10+ approved members

### 5. Priority Access to New Features
**When new features launch:**
- Founding members get access 7-14 days before regular members
- Early testing and feedback opportunity
- First to benefit from new AI tools, platform improvements
- VIP treatment for being early adopters

**Examples:**
- New AI tool launches → Founding members test first
- New resource category added → Founding members contribute first
- Platform redesign → Founding members see beta version

### 6. Direct Communication with Founder/Team
**Founding Member-Only Channels:**
- Private Slack/Discord channel (or in-platform group)
- Direct line to project owner for feedback, questions, concerns
- Monthly founder Q&A calls (first 6-12 months)
- Quarterly "State of kash_info" updates (founding members hear first)

**Why:** Founding members are partners in building the platform. Give them insider access.

### 7. Founder's Circle Recognition
**Permanent Recognition:**
- "Founding Member - [Sector] - 2025" on profile
- Listed on platform About page with permission:
  - Name
  - Industry
  - Brief bio
  - Optional: LinkedIn link, website
- Featured in launch marketing materials (with permission)

**Historical Significance:**
> "One of the original 600 elite professionals who founded kash_info in 2025."

### 8. Lifetime Benefits Lock-In
**Once you're a Founding Member, you're always a Founding Member:**
- Even if membership lapses and they rejoin later
- Benefits restore upon reactivation (within 1 year)
- Founding member status never expires
- If you were in the first 600, you're forever recognized

**Exception:** If founding member is removed for violations, founding status may be revoked (rare, case-by-case)

## FOUNDING MEMBER SELECTION PROCESS

### Who Can Become a Founding Member?

**Selection Criteria (Higher Bar Than Regular Members):**
1. **Proven Elite Status:**
   - C-level executive at known company
   - Successful entrepreneur (built/sold company)
   - Published author or recognized thought leader
   - Top 1% in their field (measurable achievements)

2. **High-Value Contribution Potential:**
   - Can bring >$1,000 worth of resources (2x regular requirement)
   - Proprietary tools, frameworks, data, or courses to share
   - Strong network of connections in their industry
   - Proven track record of helping others

3. **Platform Advocacy:**
   - Excited about kash_info vision
   - Willing to invite other elite professionals
   - Active on social media or professional networks
   - Can help spread word about the platform

4. **Cultural Fit:**
   - Growth mindset
   - Collaborative, not competitive
   - Willing to give before taking
   - Professional and respectful

### How Founding Members Are Recruited

**Months 1-2: Personal Outreach (First 200)**
- Project owner hand-picks 200 elite professionals
- Personal invitations sent (not public application)
- 1-on-1 conversations about vision
- Curated selection across all 12 sectors

**Months 3-4: Founding Member Invitations (Next 200)**
- First 200 founding members each get 5 invitations
- Can invite other elite professionals they know
- Invitees must still pass board review
- Creates network effect (elite recruiting elite)

**Months 5-6: Final Founding Spots (Last 200)**
- Remaining spots opened to applications
- "Only 200 founding spots left" campaign
- Extremely selective (highest rejection rate)
- Waitlist established if over-subscribed

**Goal:** 600 founding members by end of Month 6

## FOUNDING MEMBER COMMITMENT

### What Founding Members Must Do

**Year 1 Requirements (Higher Expectations):**
1. **Submit at least 1 major resource** worth >$497 within first 90 days
2. **Make 10+ quality posts or contributions** within first 6 months
3. **Provide platform feedback** (monthly surveys, optional feedback calls)
4. **Invite at least 3 qualified professionals** (help grow community)
5. **Maintain active engagement** (login at least 2x per week)

**Why Higher Bar?**
- Founding members set the standard
- Their contributions form the initial resource library
- Their activity creates platform momentum
- They're partners, not just members

**If Founding Member Doesn't Meet Expectations:**
- Month 3: Check-in (are you on track?)
- Month 6: Review (have you met requirements?)
- If not: Gentle nudge, offer support
- Extreme cases: May lose founding member status (keep membership but lose benefits)

## FOUNDING MEMBER FAQ

**Q: What if I join as a founding member but don't renew after Year 1?**
A: Your founding member status is preserved for 1 year. If you rejoin within 12 months, all founding benefits (including $397 pricing) are restored. After 12 months, you can rejoin but at regular member pricing ($497+).

**Q: Can I lose my founding member status?**
A: Only in extreme cases (serious policy violations, fraud, etc.). As long as you remain an active, value-contributing member, your founding status is permanent.

**Q: What if I can't afford $397/year right now?**
A: We offer a limited number of founding member scholarships (paid by other members or sponsors). Apply and explain your situation.

**Q: How are the first 200 founding members selected?**
A: Hand-picked by the project owner based on proven elite status, value contribution potential, and platform fit. This is by personal invitation only.

**Q: Can I upgrade to founding member status later?**
A: No. Founding member status is only available during Phase 1 (first 6 months, first 600 members). After that, it's closed forever.

**Q: Will there be a "Founding Board Member" tier?**
A: Yes! The 60 board members (5 per sector) are also founding members with additional privileges. Board members receive free lifetime membership for their service.
