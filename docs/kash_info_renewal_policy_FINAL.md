## CORE PRINCIPLE

**Value-First Philosophy:**
> "kash_info includes only resourceful and knowledgeable (above average) members in each industry. Members must receive value WAY MORE than the $497/year membership fee, and must contribute value WAY MORE than what they pay."

**Target Value Equation:**
- Member Pays: **$497/year**
- Member Brings: **>$497/year** (minimum)
- Member Receives: **$3,000+/year** (6x return on investment)

## RENEWAL PROCESS

### Timeline

**30 Days Before Expiry:**
- Member receives renewal notification
- Re-verification requirements explained
- Form to submit proof of value contribution over past year

**14 Days Before Expiry:**
- Invoice sent (if re-verification approved)
- Renewal link provided
- Payment deadline: Expiry date

**7 Days Before Expiry:**
- Final reminder (if payment not received)
- Warning: "Your membership will downgrade to Alumni status on [date]"

**On Expiry Date:**
- If renewed: Membership continues
- If not renewed: Downgrade to Alumni Status

---

## RENEWAL OPTIONS

### Option 1: Manual Renewal (Default)
- Member receives invoice
- Must manually pay to renew
- Must complete re-verification first

### Option 2: Auto-Renewal (Opt-In)
- Member enables in account settings
- Payment auto-charged on renewal date
- **Still requires re-verification approval**
- If re-verification fails: Auto-renewal cancelled, member notified

## RE-VERIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

### Annual Re-Verification

**Every member must demonstrate continued value contribution:**

**Submit ONE of the following:**

1. **New Major Resource** (worth >$497)
   - Course, tool, template, proprietary data, etc.
   - Board reviews quality and value

2. **Activity Summary** (if highly active)
   - 5+ high-quality posts with engagement
   - 3+ valuable resources submitted
   - 10+ meaningful contributions to discussions
   - Evidence of helping other members

3. **Network Contribution**
   - Facilitated 5+ valuable introductions
   - Brought new opportunities to members
   - Provided expert advice/consulting to members

**Auto-Approved If:**
- Member has consistently brought >$497 value throughout year
- Board members vouch for their contributions
- Clear evidence of positive community impact

**Manual Review If:**
- Activity below threshold
- New resource submitted for evaluation
- Unclear value contribution

**Rejected If:**
- Inactive for 6+ months
- No demonstrable value contribution
- Resources/contributions deemed below standard
- Behavioral issues or community guideline violations

## BOARD MEMBER REVIEW SYSTEM

### Structure

**Board Composition:**
- **5 Board Members per Industry Sector**
- Each board member is an expert in their domain
- Board members review applications/renewals in their sector

**Industry Sectors:**
1. Tech/SaaS/Engineering
2. Marketing/Growth/Sales
3. Finance/Investment/Banking
4. Creative/Design/Media
5. Healthcare/Medical
6. Real Estate/Property
7. E-commerce/Retail
8. Consulting/Strategy
9. Legal/Law
10. Education/EdTech
11. Manufacturing/Supply Chain
12. [Reserved for future expansion]

**Total Board Members:** 60 board members (12 sectors × 5 members each)

**Initial Launch:** May start with 5-7 key sectors, expanding to all 12 as membership grows

### Board Member Responsibilities

1. **Review New Applications** (in their industry)
2. **Review Annual Re-Verifications** (for members in their sector)
3. **Evaluate Resource Quality** (submitted by members in their domain)
4. **Handle Appeals** (first rejection appeals)
5. **Maintain Quality Standards** (ensure "above average" threshold)

### Board Member Selection

**Initial Board (Founding):**
- Hand-picked by project owner
- Must be elite in their field
- Proven track record of expertise
- Strong judgment of quality

**Board Member Benefits:**
- Free lifetime membership
- Special "Board Member" badge on profile
- Early access to new features
- Recognition as industry authority
- [OPTIONAL: Revenue share from approved members in their sector]

**Term Length:**
- 1-year terms (renewable)
- Can be removed for inactivity or poor judgment
- Can nominate replacement when stepping down

### Review Process

**New Application Review:**
1. Application submitted
2. System routes to relevant industry board (based on applicant's sector)
3. Assigned to 1-2 board members in that sector
4. Board member(s) review:
   - Professional background
   - Value contribution potential
   - Resources they can bring
   - Fit with community
5. Decision: **Approve** / **Reject** / **Request More Info**
6. Timeline: **7 days maximum** for board decision

**Voting System:**
- Single reviewer: Their decision is final
- Two reviewers: Both must approve (or 1 approve + owner tiebreaker)
- Three+ reviewers: Majority vote

**Annual Re-Verification Review:**
- Same process as new applications
- Board evaluates: Did member deliver on value promise?
- Faster review (members already vetted, just confirming continued value)

## APPEAL PROCESS

### Right to Appeal

**If Application/Renewal is Rejected:**
- Member has the right to **appeal ONCE**

**Appeal Window:**
- Must submit appeal within **14 days** of rejection notice
- After 14 days, appeal window closes

**Appeal Requirements:**
- Written explanation of why they should be reconsidered
- Additional evidence of value contribution
- New resources or references not included in original application
- Cannot simply resubmit same application

**Appeal Review:**
- Reviewed by **different board member** in same sector (fresh perspective)
- OR reviewed by **senior board member / project owner** if no other board member available
- Timeline: **7 days** for appeal decision
- Decision is **FINAL** (no second appeal)

**Appeal Outcomes:**

1. **Appeal Approved:**
   - Member can renew / join
   - Pay membership fee
   - Access granted

2. **Appeal Rejected:**
   - Member cannot renew / join
   - Must wait **101 days** before reapplying
   - Reapplication treated as new applicant (fresh review)

### 101-Day Reapplication Rule

**Why 101 days?**

kash_info uses a **101-day waiting period** as a meaningful timeframe for growth and improvement. This period has multiple interpretations, allowing us to frame rejections appropriately based on context:

**Primary Philosophy: "The Level Up Period"**
- 101 days ≈ 14.4 weeks ≈ 3.3 months
- Time to complete a professional certification
- Time to build a substantial portfolio project
- Time to develop a new high-value skill
- Time to create a major resource worth >$497

**Alternative Framing: "1% Better Every Day"**
- 101 days = 100 days of improvement + 1 day of application
- Inspired by compound growth philosophy (1% better daily = 2.7x improvement)
- 100 days to improve, Day 101 to prove it

**Practical Benefits:**
- Prevents immediate spam reapplications
- Gives meaningful time for skill development
- Aligns with business quarter cycles (~90 days)
- Long enough to demonstrate commitment
- Short enough to maintain interest

**After 101 Days:**
- Member can submit new application
- Must show significant improvement from previous rejection
- Treated as new applicant (full review process)
- Must be invited again (by active member or project owner)

## ALUMNI STATUS (Expired Members)

### What is Alumni Status?

When a member's subscription expires (and they don't renew), they are downgraded to **Alumni Status**.

**Alumni Status = Read-Only Access**

### Alumni Permissions

**✅ Alumni CAN:**
- View posts and discussions
- Browse resource library
- See member profiles
- View their own profile (with "Alumni" badge)
- Receive newsletter/announcements (if opted in)

**❌ Alumni CANNOT:**
- Create posts or comments
- Message members (direct messages disabled)
- Use AI tools (chat, SEO, content creation, research)
- Submit resources
- Request resources
- Download resources (read-only, no downloads)
- Invite new members
- Vote or engage with content

**Visual Indicator:**
- Profile badge: "Alumni Member (Inactive)"
- Greyed-out UI elements for disabled features
- Banner: "Your membership expired on [date]. Renew to regain full access."

## REACTIVATION FROM ALUMNI STATUS

### Scenario 1: Recent Alumni (Within 6 Months)

**Process:**
1. Alumni requests reactivation
2. Submit light re-verification:
   - What value will you bring upon renewal?
   - Brief update on what you've been working on
   - At least 1 new resource or contribution plan
3. Board reviews (faster process, already vetted member)
4. If approved: Pay renewal fee → Access restored immediately
5. All prior data restored (posts, messages, resources)

**Timeline:** 3-5 days for review

---

### Scenario 2: Long-Inactive Alumni (6+ Months)

**Process:**
1. Alumni must go through **full application process** again
2. Requires new invitation (from active member or project owner)
3. Fill out complete application form
4. Board reviews as if new applicant
5. Higher bar: Must show significant improvement or new value
6. If approved: Pay membership fee → Prior data restored

**Timeline:** 7-14 days for full review

---

### Scenario 3: Rejected at Re-Verification

**Process:**
1. Member cannot reactivate immediately
2. Must wait **101 days** (same as appeal rejection)
3. After 101 days: Can reapply with new invitation
4. Must demonstrate significant improvement in value contribution
5. Full application review by board

## GRACE PERIODS

### Payment Grace Period

**If payment fails on renewal date:**
- 7-day grace period to update payment method
- Member retains full access during grace period
- Reminders sent on day 1, 3, 5, 7
- After 7 days: Downgrade to alumni status

**Conditions:**
- Re-verification must already be approved
- Only applies to payment issues (card declined, etc.)
- Does NOT apply if member is actively choosing not to renew

### Re-Verification Grace Period

**If re-verification is pending:**
- No grace period (must be submitted before expiry)
- Warning sent 30 days before: "Submit re-verification by [date]"
- Reminder at 14 days, 7 days, 1 day
- If not submitted by expiry: Automatic downgrade to alumni

**Exception:**
- If member has been highly active and valuable, owner can grant 14-day extension (case-by-case)

## MEMBERSHIP CAPACITY (Q2 ANSWERED)

### Maximum Members: 3,600 Total

**Structure:**
- **300 members maximum per sector**
- **12 sectors = 3,600 total members at full capacity**

**Why 300 per sector?**
- Ultra-exclusive: Top 300 professionals globally in each industry
- Intimate enough for real connections within your sector
- Large enough for diverse resources and network effects
- Creates meaningful scarcity ("one of only 300")
- Manageable community size for quality maintenance

**Positioning:**
> "Join the top 300 [industry] professionals in the world. When we reach capacity, the only way in is through the waitlist."

### Phased Growth Strategy

**Phase 1: Founding Members (Months 1-6)**
- **50 members per sector** (600 total)
- Hand-picked elite professionals
- Early adopter pricing: $397/year
- Special "Founding Member" status
- Build core community and gather feedback

**Phase 2: Controlled Expansion (Months 7-18)**
- **150 members per sector** (1,800 total)
- Invitation-based growth
- Standard pricing: $497/year
- Full board review operational
- Platform features mature

**Phase 3: Selective Growth to Capacity (Months 19-36)**
- **300 members per sector** (3,600 maximum)
- Extremely selective approvals
- Waitlists established per sector
- Premium pricing: $597-697/year (demand-driven)
- Platform fully scaled

**Phase 4: Waitlist & Attrition (Year 3+)**
- Capacity reached
- Waitlist-only admission (when members leave)
- Strict annual re-verification maintains quality
- Price adjusts based on demand per sector

**If demand varies significantly by sector:**
- Allow sector caps to flex within overall 3,600 limit
- Minimum per sector: 200 members
- Maximum per sector: 400 members
- Market demand dictates sector distribution

**Example flexible scenario:**
- Tech: 400 (high demand)

- Healthcare: 250 (moderate)
- Manufacturing: 200 (slower adoption)

### Waitlist Management

**When sector reaches 300 capacity:**
1. Applications still accepted but go to waitlist
2. Waitlist ranked by:
   - Application quality score
   - Value contribution potential
   - Date applied
3. When member leaves (non-renewal, removal):
   - Next person on waitlist gets invitation
   - Must still pass board review
   - Has 7 days to accept or spot goes to next

**Waitlist Benefits:**
- Creates urgency ("get on the waitlist before it's too long")
- Members are incentivized to renew (hard to get back in)
- Maintains quality (still requires approval, not automatic)

- 3,600 members × $497/year = **$1,788,000 annual revenue**
