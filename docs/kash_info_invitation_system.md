**Date:** 2025-10-26
**Status:** Q4 Answered - Approved
**Applies To:** Regular members (active), Founding members have unlimited invites

---

## INVITATION QUOTA SYSTEM

### Initial Allocation

**Regular Members:**
- Start with **5 invitation credits**
- Each approved invitation earns **5 more credits**
- Credits accumulate (no cap on total invitations if quality is good)
- Rejected invitations trigger warning system (see below)

**Founding Members:**
- **Unlimited invitation credits**
- Still tracked for quality score and badges
- Same consequence system applies (warnings, not quota limits)

---

## INVITATION REPLENISHMENT SYSTEM

### How Credits Work

**Starting Point:**
- New member joins → Gets 5 invitation credits immediately

**Earning More Credits:**
- Send invitation → Board approves applicant → **+5 credits added**
- Credits are cumulative (if you have 3 left and get +5, you now have 8)

**Example Flow:**
```
Member joins:                    5 credits
Invites Person A (approved):     10 credits (+5)
Invites Person B (approved):     15 credits (+5)
Invites Person C (rejected):     15 credits (no change, but warning issued)
Invites Person D (approved):     20 credits (+5)
```

**Why This Works:**
- Rewards quality recruiters (more credits = more invites)
- High-quality members can recruit entire networks
- Poor-quality inviters naturally limited (no credit growth)
- Self-regulating system (good behavior = more power)

## QUALITY SCORE SYSTEM

### How Quality Score is Calculated

**Starting Score:** 50 (neutral)

**Score Changes:**
- ✅ **Approved Invitation:** +10 points
- ❌ **Rejected Invitation:** -5 points
- 🎖️ **Invited member becomes highly active (Year 1):** Bonus +5 points
- 🏆 **Invited member becomes founding member of another sector:** Bonus +10 points
- ⚠️ **Invited member removed for violations (within 1 year):** -15 points

**Score Ranges:**

| Score | Status | Meaning | Effects |
|-------|--------|---------|---------|
| 90-100 | Elite Recruiter 🏆 | Exceptional quality | Bonus perks, special badge |
| 70-89 | Excellent 🌟 | High quality invitations | Normal privileges |
| 50-69 | Good ✅ | Average quality | Normal privileges |
| 30-49 | Needs Improvement ⚠️ | Below average | Warning issued |
| 0-29 | Poor ❌ | Low quality | Invitation privileges suspended |

**Score Display:**
- Shown on profile as number + visual (stars, emoji, color)
- Updates in real-time after each invitation decision

## WARNING & CONSEQUENCE SYSTEM

### Rejection Tracking

**Strikes System:**

**After 1st Rejection:**
- No consequence
- Email: "Your invitation for [Name] was not approved. Board feedback: [reason]"

**After 2nd Rejection:**
- No consequence
- Email: "This is your 2nd rejected invitation. Please ensure future invitees meet our standards: [link to criteria]"

**After 3rd Rejection:**
- ⚠️ **Warning Issued**
- Email: "You've had 3 rejected invitations. Please review our approval criteria carefully. After 2 more rejections, your invitation privileges will be paused."
- Quality score drops to "Needs Improvement" range

**After 4th Rejection:**
- ⚠️ **Final Warning**
- Email: "This is your final warning. One more rejected invitation will result in a 30-day pause on invitation privileges."

**After 5th Rejection:**
- 🚫 **Invitation Privileges Suspended (30 days)**
- Email: "Your invitation privileges have been paused for 30 days due to 5 rejected invitations. During this time, focus on understanding our quality standards. Your privileges will automatically restore on [date]."
- Cannot send invitations for 30 days
- Quality score in "Poor" range

**After Suspension Ends:**
- Invitation privileges restored
- Quota returns to previous level
- Rejection counter resets to 0 (fresh start)
- Quality score slowly recovers with good invitations

---

### Reset Mechanism (Redemption Path)

**How to Recover from Warnings:**

**Option 1: Successful Approvals Reset Counter**
- Every approved invitation reduces rejection count by 1
- Example:
  - 3 rejections (warning issued)
  - 1 approval → rejection count drops to 2 (warning removed)
  - Another approval → rejection count drops to 1

**Option 2: Time-Based Reset**
- Rejection counter resets to 0 after 6 months of no rejections
- Gives members a clean slate

**Option 3: Training Completion**
- Complete "Quality Invitation Training" (5-min video + quiz)
- Reduces rejection count by 1
- Can only be done once per 6 months

## FOUNDING MEMBER EXCEPTION

### Unlimited Invitations (But Still Tracked)

**Founding Members Get:**
- ✅ **Unlimited invitation credits** (no quota limit)
- ✅ **Still earn quality score** (tracked for recognition)
- ✅ **Still earn badges** (achievement system applies)
- ✅ **Still face consequences** for poor quality invitations

**Consequence Differences:**
- No quota suspension (they have unlimited anyway)
- But after 5 rejections: **Personal review by owner**
  - "Hey, I noticed you've had 5 rejections recently. Let's chat about what we're looking for."
  - Coaching conversation, not punishment
  - Extreme cases: May lose founding member benefits (rare)

**Why Track Founding Members:**
- Maintain quality standards
- Recognize high performers
- Ensure founding members set good example

```
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

- Fills out full application form
- Submits for board review
- Status: "Under Board Review"

**6. Board Reviews**
- Board members see: "Invited by [Your Name] (Quality Score: 85)"
- Review application based on criteria
- Approve or Reject

**7. Outcome Notification**

**If Approved:**
- Email to inviter: "Great news! [Name] has been approved. You've earned +5 invitation credits and +10 quality score points!"
- Invitation credit restored + 5 bonus credits
- Quality score increases
- Badge progress updated

**If Rejected:**
- Email to inviter: "Your invitation for [Name] was not approved. Board feedback: [reason]. Your quality score has been adjusted."
- Invitation credit NOT restored (used up)
- Quality score decreases
- Rejection counter increases
- Warning system triggers (if applicable)
