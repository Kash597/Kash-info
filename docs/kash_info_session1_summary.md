## ✅ QUESTIONS ANSWERED

### Q1: Membership Renewal & Access ✅

**DECISION:**
- **Renewal process:** 30/14/7 day notifications
- **Options:** Manual renewal (default) or auto-renewal (opt-in)
- **Expired members:** Downgrade to "Alumni Status" (read-only access)
- **Re-verification:** Annual proof of >$497 value contribution required
- **Board review:** 60 board members (5 per sector × 12 sectors) review all applications
- **Appeal process:** One appeal allowed, 14-day window, reviewed by different board member
- **101-day rule:** Must wait 101 days after rejection to reapply ("Level Up" period)
- **Rejection templates:** 6 different templates based on scenario

**KEY INNOVATION: Board Member Review System**
- Industry expert peer review (not just owner)
- 12 sectors: Tech, Marketing, Finance, Creative, Healthcare, Real Estate, E-commerce, Consulting, Legal, Education, Manufacturing, + 1 reserved
- Distributed quality control at scale

### Q2: Maximum Member Capacity ✅

**DECISION:**
- **Ultra-exclusive model:** 300 members per sector maximum
- **Total capacity:** 3,600 members (12 sectors × 300)
- **Phased growth:**
  - Phase 1 (Months 1-6): 600 founding members (50/sector)
  - Phase 2 (Months 7-18): 1,800 total (150/sector)
  - Phase 3 (Months 19-36): 3,600 total (300/sector)
  - Phase 4 (Year 3+): Waitlist only, attrition-based admission

**Positioning:**
> "Join the top 300 [industry] professionals in the world."

**Waitlist system:**
- When sector hits 300, applications go to waitlist
- Members who leave = spot opens for next on waitlist
- Ranked by quality, value potential, date applied

**Revenue Projections:**
- Phase 1: $238,200/year (600 founding @ $397)
- Phase 2: $834,600/year (600 founding + 1,200 regular)
- Phase 3: $1.73M - $2.33M/year (at capacity, depending on pricing)

**Dynamic capacity option:** Allow 200-400 per sector based on demand (total still ~3,600)

### Q3: Founding Members ✅

**DECISION: Option A - Full Benefits Package**

**Founding Member Definition:**
- First 600 members (50 per sector)
- Highest quality bar (hand-picked elite)
- Partners in building the platform

**Benefits Package:**
1. 🏆 **Founding Member badge** (permanent recognition)
2. 💰 **$397/year forever** (regular: $497-697+, locked-in grandfather pricing)
3. 🎯 **Lighter re-verification** (annual check-in vs. full review)
4. 🔓 **Unlimited invitations** (regular members: max 5)
5. ⚡ **Priority access** to new features (7-14 days early)
6. 💬 **Direct communication** with founder/team
7. 📜 **Permanent recognition** on About page and profile
8. 🔐 **Lifetime status** (never expires, even if membership lapses temporarily)

**3-Phase Founding Member Recruitment:**
- **Phase 1 (Months 1-2):** 200 hand-picked by owner (personal invitations)
- **Phase 2 (Months 3-4):** 200 via founding member invitations (first 200 recruit next 200)
- **Phase 3 (Months 5-6):** 200 via open applications ("last chance")

**Founding member expectations (Year 1):**
- Submit 1+ major resource (>$497 value) within 90 days
- Make 10+ quality posts within 6 months
- Provide platform feedback
- Invite at least 3 qualified professionals
- Maintain active engagement (2x/week minimum)

**Program closes:** Month 7 (founding status never available again)

   - Annual re-verification maintains standards
